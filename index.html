One appointment. A whole new arch.

A full‑arch restoration, start to finish.

Intake

Every case starts with a photographic and tactile record of what is actually there: enamel staining, chipped incisal edges, a missing lower molar, plaque along the gingival margin, and early gingival recession.

Diagnostics

A single low‑dose radiographic sweep maps roots, canals and bone before treatment planning. Typical findings on a case like this one: caries at #14, a fracture at #19, and an extraction site at #30 awaiting restoration.

Treatment

Work runs left to right across the arch in five passes — ultrasonic scaling, caries removal with a high‑speed handpiece, composite placement, light curing, and polishing — so the whole arch is completed in a single sitting.

Reveal

The finished arch is shade‑matched and contoured, then closed into occlusion and checked from every angle before you leave the chair.
